史密鼠｜教檢教甄刷題 - Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026
Application package: com.aicoconfs.teacherexam
Developer / Operator: aicoconfs

This Privacy Policy explains how 史密鼠｜教檢教甄刷題 (the “App”) handles user data.

The App provides teacher certification and teacher recruitment exam practice, daily recommendations, answer history, lightweight learning memory, progress views, and question-bank updates. Except for network access required to deliver question-bank updates, the App is designed to keep its core learning data on the user’s device.

1. No account is required

The App currently:

  • Does not require account registration or sign-in.
  • Does not use third-party sign-in.
  • Does not ask users to provide a name, phone number, postal address, government identification number, or other account identity information.

2. Data stored locally on the device

To provide learning and practice features, the App may store the following information locally on the user’s device:

  • Question IDs.
  • Answer results and answer history.
  • Answer times or related timestamps.
  • Learning-memory state, such as answer performance, review status, correct-answer streaks, and next-review timing.
  • Daily recommendation results and the local learning state used to generate them.
  • Learning progress and calendar-style progress information.
  • Downloaded or cached question-bank content.
  • Question-bank version information, update state, and local cache pointers.

These learning records are currently used only to provide features within the App. The App does not upload them to the developer’s servers to create a user learning profile, perform advertising analytics, or track users across devices.

3. Question-bank updates and network connections

The App may connect over HTTPS to the following content host:

https://teacher-exam-lite-content.pages.dev

This connection is used to:

  • Check whether a newer question-bank release is available.
  • Download question-bank content and release metadata.
  • Verify and activate downloaded question-bank updates.

The App does not intentionally include the user’s answer history, learning-memory state, progress data, or daily recommendation data in these question-bank update requests.

However, when a device connects to an Internet service, infrastructure providers involved in network delivery, hosting, or CDN services may process ordinary technical connection data as part of providing the service. This may include information such as IP address, request time, request path, client information, or other standard network metadata. Actual processing may depend on the relevant service provider’s policies and infrastructure configuration.

We do not use the question-bank hosting service to create individual learning profiles, and we do not use users’ answer records for advertising or cross-service tracking.

4. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App currently:

  • Does not contain third-party advertising.
  • Does not use behavioral advertising.
  • Does not use third-party analytics SDKs to track users’ learning behavior.
  • Does not create advertising identifiers for cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • Does not sell users’ learning data.

5. Device permissions

The App currently requires only network-related capability in order to check for and download question-bank updates.

The App currently does not request access to the following sensitive device data:

  • Precise location.
  • Contacts.
  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Photo or media library.
  • SMS or call logs.
  • Health or medical data.

If a future version introduces functionality that requires additional permissions, this Privacy Policy and the relevant Google Play disclosures will be updated to reflect the actual behavior.

6. Data retention and deletion

The App does not provide user accounts, so there is currently no server-side account data that requires an account-deletion request.

Local learning data is generally retained on the user’s device so that learning progress can be preserved. Users can delete local App data by:

  • Clearing the App’s storage / application data in Android system settings.
  • Uninstalling the App.

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are currently disabled for the App to reduce the possibility of local learning data being automatically backed up or transferred by the operating system.

7. Data security

Remote question-bank updates use HTTPS. Before downloaded question-bank content becomes active, the App performs release, structure, and integrity validation.

We use reasonable technical measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage or network transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-party services

The App may rely on third-party network infrastructure to deliver question-bank content and HTTPS traffic, including hosting or CDN services.

Such third-party services may process necessary technical connection data in accordance with their own privacy policies, terms, and infrastructure settings. This Privacy Policy does not replace the privacy policies of those third-party services.

9. Minors

The App is primarily intended for users aged 18 and older who are preparing for teacher certification or teacher recruitment examinations.

The App is not primarily directed to children and does not require users to provide account or identity information.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

If future versions add accounts, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising, payments, third-party sign-in, new sensitive permissions, or other features that materially change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

Material changes may also be announced within the App, on the store listing, or on this policy page.
