史密鼠 – Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how “史密鼠” (the “App”) accesses, uses, stores, and processes data related to its users.

The App is developed by aicoconfs and is primarily designed for practicing past teacher recruitment examination questions. Its features include Today’s Practice, Free Practice, past exam practice, answer history, learning progress, question bank updates, question search, and question-related tools.

The App’s primary learning features operate locally on the user’s device. Answer history, learning state, and learning progress are not uploaded to the developer’s servers and are not used for advertising or behavioral analytics.

1. No Account Is Required

The App currently:

  • Does not require users to register or sign in.
  • Does not use Google, Facebook, or other third-party account sign-in services.
  • Does not require users to provide their name, phone number, address, national identification number, or other account-identifying information.
  • Does not provide cloud-based account synchronization.
  • Does not upload users’ learning history to a developer account or personal cloud account.

As a result, the App does not maintain server-side learning data linked to a user account.

2. Data Stored Locally on the Device

To provide question practice, learning-memory features, progress tracking, and the ability to resume interrupted practice sessions, the App may store the following data locally on the user’s device:

  • Question identifiers, subject identifiers, and related question bank identifiers.
  • Answer outcomes and answer history.
  • Answer timestamps and local time information used to correctly attribute learning activity to a study day.
  • Cumulative attempt counts, correct and incorrect answer history, correct-answer streak state, and review scheduling information.
  • Learning progress and local state required to calculate progress from answer history.
  • Unfinished practice-session information, such as practice mode, fixed question order, current position, submitted-question state, and score.
  • Question bank version identifiers and related content-version information.
  • Downloaded and validated question bank files and required content assets.
  • The user’s selected light or dark display theme.
  • The user’s sound-feedback preference.
  • Local submission-identification information used to maintain data consistency and prevent the same answer submission from being recorded more than once.

This information is mainly used to:

  • Preserve the user’s answer history.
  • Calculate daily activity and learning progress.
  • Schedule questions that should be reviewed again.
  • Allow users to resume an interrupted practice session.
  • Prevent duplicate counting of the same submission.
  • Restore required local state after the App is restarted.

Today’s Practice scheduling, learning progress, and related statistics are primarily calculated from the question bank and learning records stored on the device.

These learning records are not transmitted to the developer, the question bank host, or third-party analytics services as a result of ordinary question-answering activity.

3. Question Bank Updates and Network Connections

The App may connect over HTTPS to the official question bank service:

https://teacher-exam-lite-content.pages.dev

The question bank service currently uses Cloudflare Pages to deliver content.

These connections may be used to:

  • Check whether a question bank version is available.
  • Retrieve question bank version information.
  • Download updated question bank content and required assets.
  • Validate the version, size, structure, and integrity of downloaded content.
  • Activate a new question bank only after successful validation.

If a question bank update fails, the App is designed to preserve an existing valid question bank whenever possible instead of replacing it with incomplete or invalid content.

Question bank update requests do not intentionally include:

  • The user’s selected answers.
  • Answer history.
  • Learning progress.
  • Review schedules.
  • Today’s Practice results.
  • Answer details from unfinished practice sessions.
  • Display-theme preferences.
  • Sound preferences.

When the device connects to the question bank service, internet service providers, hosting providers, or CDN providers may process ordinary network metadata required to provide the service, such as:

  • IP address.
  • Request time.
  • Request path.
  • Browser or network-client technical information.
  • Other technical information normally required to provide HTTPS network services.

Such network metadata is processed by the relevant network or hosting providers in accordance with their own terms of service and privacy policies.

4. Question Search and Google

When the user explicitly selects “Search Question”, the App:

  1. Obtains the text of the current question stem.
  2. Normalizes whitespace.
  3. Limits the search text to a maximum of 180 characters.
  4. Creates a Google Search URL.
  5. Uses Android’s external-opening mechanism to hand the URL to an external browser on the device.

The App itself does not directly send a Google Search HTTP request.

However, when the external browser opens the search URL, the search text is transmitted by that browser to Google.

The search text may contain part of the current question stem. The App does not intentionally add the following information to the search request:

  • The user’s selected answer.
  • Correct or incorrect answer history.
  • Learning progress.
  • Review schedules.
  • Unfinished practice-session state.
  • Display-theme preferences.
  • Sound settings.

This transfer occurs only after the user explicitly chooses to search the question.

How the external browser and Google process the search text and related connection information depends on the user’s browser settings and Google’s Privacy Policy:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Question Information and Issue Reporting

The App may provide “Question Information” and “Report an Issue” features to help users verify question-source information or report problems with a question to the developer.

Question Information may display available information such as:

  • Year.
  • Organizing authority or examination source.
  • Subject.
  • Original question number.
  • Exam paper information.

When the user explicitly uses the “Report an Issue” feature, the App generates a limited diagnostic report locally on the device. The report may include:

  • App version.
  • Question bank release identifier.
  • Question identifier.
  • Exam paper identifier, if available.
  • Year.
  • Organizing authority.
  • Subject.
  • Original question number.
  • The issue category selected by the user.
  • Additional text voluntarily entered by the user.

The issue report does not automatically include:

  • The user’s answer history.
  • Learning progress.
  • Review schedule.
  • LearningMemory or other local learning-memory data.
  • The user’s answers to other questions.
  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Account information.

The App does not automatically send an issue report to the developer’s server.

Only after the user explicitly chooses to report an issue will the App attempt to open an external email application on the device and provide the prepared report for the user to review and send.

Once an email is actually sent, the data is processed by the user’s chosen email application and email service provider according to their respective privacy policies.

If no compatible email application is available, the App may allow the user to copy the prepared report text to the system clipboard so that the user can decide how to use it.

6. Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

The App itself currently:

  • Does not contain third-party advertising.
  • Does not use behavioral advertising.
  • Does not contain third-party app analytics SDKs.
  • Does not contain third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Does not use Firebase Analytics.
  • Does not use Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Does not create cross-app or cross-website advertising tracking identifiers.
  • Does not use the Android Advertising ID for tracking.
  • Does not sell user data.
  • Does not provide users’ learning data to advertisers.
  • Does not use answer history to create advertising or behavioral-analysis profiles.

Sound feedback, haptic feedback, display-theme changes, and other ordinary interface actions do not cause user activity to be transmitted to analytics services.

Analytics on This Privacy Policy Website

This Privacy Policy webpage and the Android App operate in separate technical environments.

This policy webpage is currently hosted through GitHub Pages and may load Google Analytics for website-traffic statistics.

Depending on the website configuration and the user’s browser settings, Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may process information such as:

  • Website visit information.
  • IP address.
  • Browser information.
  • Device-related technical information.

This website analytics functionality:

  • Does not run inside the 史密鼠 Android App.
  • Does not directly access answer history stored inside the App.
  • Does not directly access LearningMemory.
  • Does not directly access learning progress stored inside the App.

For information about how Google processes such data, please refer to:

Google Privacy Policy

7. Device Permissions

The App currently uses general network connectivity to support:

  • Question bank version checks and downloads.
  • External question searches explicitly initiated by the user.
  • Opening the external Privacy Policy webpage.
  • Other external webpage or email actions explicitly initiated by the user.

The App currently does not request or use the following sensitive permissions for its primary functionality:

  • Precise or approximate location.
  • Contacts.
  • Camera.
  • Microphone.
  • Photos or media library.
  • SMS.
  • Call logs.
  • Health or medical data.
  • Advertising ID permission.

The App does not request these sensitive permissions merely because the user answers questions.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not use user accounts and therefore does not maintain server-side learning data linked to an account that must be deleted through an account-deletion request.

Local learning data generally remains on the user’s device until the user chooses to remove it.

Users may delete App data using Android system controls, including:

  • Clearing the App’s storage or application data in Android system settings.
  • Uninstalling the App.

These actions may delete locally stored information including:

  • Answer history.
  • Learning-memory data.
  • Learning progress.
  • Unfinished practice sessions.
  • Cached question bank content.
  • Display and sound preferences.

Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are currently disabled for the App. Accordingly, local learning data is not automatically transferred to another device through the App’s Android backup configuration.

9. Data Security

Remote question bank content is transmitted over HTTPS.

Before newly downloaded question bank content is activated, the App performs the applicable validation procedures supported by the content format, including:

  • Version validation.
  • File-size validation.
  • SHA-256 or equivalent integrity validation.
  • Data-structure validation.
  • Question bank content validation.

A new question bank is activated only after the required validation succeeds.

If a download or activation process fails, the App is designed to preserve an existing valid question bank whenever possible so that incomplete or corrupted content does not replace usable content.

We use reasonable technical measures intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, corruption, or misuse of data.

However, no method of electronic storage or network transmission can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

The App or this Privacy Policy webpage may currently involve the following third-party services:

Cloudflare Pages

Used to host and deliver question bank update content.

When the user’s device makes an HTTPS request to the question bank service, Cloudflare or related network-service providers may process ordinary connection metadata required to deliver the service.

Google Search

Used only when the user explicitly selects “Search Question.”

The search text is handed to Google through an external browser.

External Email Applications and Email Services

Used only after the user explicitly chooses to report a question issue and chooses to open an email application.

The prepared issue-report content is then handed to the external email application. Actual email transmission and subsequent processing are handled by the email service selected by the user.

GitHub Pages

Used to host this Privacy Policy webpage.

Google Analytics

May be used to measure visits to this Privacy Policy website.

Google Analytics is not embedded in the 史密鼠 Android App itself.

Third-party services may process necessary information in accordance with their own privacy policies, terms of service, and technical configurations.

11. Minors

The App is primarily intended for users aged 18 or older who are preparing for teacher recruitment examinations.

The App:

  • Is not primarily directed at children.
  • Does not require users to create an account.
  • Does not require users to provide their name, phone number, address, or other personal identification information.
  • Does not use behavioral advertising or advertising tracking directed at children.

If the App’s features, third-party services, or data-processing practices change materially in the future, we may update this Privacy Policy.

For example, we would update this policy and the relevant Google Play Data Safety disclosures if the App later adds:

  • User accounts.
  • Cloud synchronization.
  • Analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Advertising services.
  • New data-transmission features.
  • Other features that materially change how user data is processed.

Material changes may be communicated through:

  • The App.
  • The Google Play Store listing.
  • This Privacy Policy webpage.
